Stays on your computer.

Works offline. No accounts, no tracking, nothing sent anywhere. It only goes online when you select the version number to check for updates.

Your photos stay untouched.

Decklet never moves, renames or changes your photos. Delete moves a folder into _deleted, so nothing is removed for good.

Just a folder of photos.

Each person is a folder with a front and a back photo. Open it in any file browser, back it up, or take it to another computer.

Every card you've kept,
where you can find it.

Front and back always line up.

Decklet finds the edges of the card in each photo, straightens it and crops away the table. Both sides come out the same size, even when the photos were taken differently. Missed an edge? Drag the corners into place.

Copy in one click

Copy any email, phone number or address, or the whole card as a vCard for your contacts app.

Add a card in seconds

Drop the front and back photos, type the name and select Save card. Your original photos stay where they are.

Photos from your phone

Use .jpg, .png, .webp or .heic photos, straight from the camera.

Keyboard first

Where you met

Moves with you

First time opening it? Here's how

Decklet isn't notarised by Apple yet, so macOS shows a warning the first time. This is expected.

  1. Open Decklet and click Done on the warning.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message saying “Decklet” was blocked, and click Open Anyway.
  3. Enter your password, then click Open. You only need to do this once.
